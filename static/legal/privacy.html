Privacy Policy

Prepared in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

Effective date: 12 August 2026 · malita-maths.streamlit.app

Malita (Pty) Ltd ("Malita", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of learners and other users of our AI-powered mathematics tutoring platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have in relation to it, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions. By using the Service, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal information as described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information: full name, email address, password (stored in encrypted/hashed form, never in plain text), school name (optional), province, and city/town, provided when you register.
  • Usage information: your subscription tier, daily AI Tutor usage counts, questions submitted to the AI Tutor and Practice Questions, and progress/marks data, generated as you use the Service.
  • Uploaded content: images and PDF documents you choose to upload for OCR scanning or past-paper extraction, processed to provide that feature.
  • Payment-related references: a payment reference ID and subscription status linking your account to a transaction processed by PayFast. We do not collect or store your card number, expiry date, or CVV — these are entered directly on PayFast's own secure payment page and never pass through Malita's systems.
  • Technical information: standard web request information (such as IP address and browser type) collected automatically by our hosting providers for security and reliability purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account, and authenticate you when you log in;
  • Provide the Service, including generating AI Tutor solutions, grading practice questions, and processing uploaded documents/images;
  • Process subscription payments and manage your subscription tier, working together with PayFast;
  • Enforce Free Plan usage limits and unlock paid features once payment is confirmed;
  • Understand usage patterns by province and city/town, in aggregate, to improve the Service and inform where to focus our efforts;
  • Communicate with you about your account, billing, or changes to the Service;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Service, including detecting fraud or abuse;
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

4. Our Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following bases recognised under POPIA:

  • Consent, given by you (or your parent/guardian, where applicable) when you register for an account;
  • Necessity for the performance of a contract, namely providing the Service you have signed up for and processing payment for a subscription you have chosen;
  • Compliance with a legal obligation, such as retaining certain records for tax or regulatory purposes;
  • Our legitimate interests, such as maintaining the security of the Service, provided this does not override your own rights and interests.

5. Children's Personal Information

Malita is used primarily by Grade 12 learners, some of whom are under the age of 18. POPIA places additional restrictions on the processing of children's personal information. Where a user is under 18:

  • We rely on the consent of a parent or legal guardian (a "competent person" under POPIA) to process that learner's personal information, which we require be obtained before registration, as set out in our Terms and Conditions;
  • We collect only the personal information reasonably necessary to provide the Service (see Section 2), and do not use children's personal information for unrelated marketing purposes;
  • Parents or guardians may contact us at any time using the details in Section 12 to review, correct, or request deletion of their child's personal information.

We do not sell your personal information. We share personal information only with the following categories of third parties, each engaged to help us operate the Service:

7. Cross-Border Transfer of Information

Some of our service providers (including our database and application hosting providers) may store or process personal information on servers located outside South Africa. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure such providers apply a level of protection for personal information that is at least equivalent to the protections required under POPIA, including through the use of reputable providers with their own security and compliance commitments.

8. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure, including:

  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing (bcrypt) and are never stored or visible in plain text, including to Malita staff;
  • Payment card details are never collected or stored by Malita — all card data is handled exclusively by PayFast, a PCI DSS-compliant payment provider;
  • Access to our production database and hosting infrastructure is restricted and protected by credentials known only to authorised personnel;
  • Data submitted for verification of automated payment notifications (webhooks) is cryptographically verified before any account or subscription change is made.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and while we work to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account remains active, and thereafter for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations (such as financial record-keeping requirements). If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required or permitted by law to retain it for longer.

10. Your Rights

Subject to POPIA, you (or, for a minor, your parent or guardian) have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
  • Request deletion or destruction of personal information we no longer have a lawful basis to retain;
  • Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances;
  • Withdraw consent to processing, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (see Section 13).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12. We may need to verify your identity before actioning certain requests.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Where changes are material, we will provide reasonable notice (such as an email or an in-app notice) before they take effect. The "Effective date" at the top of this Policy indicates when it was last updated.
