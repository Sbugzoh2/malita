Delete Your Malita Account

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and personal data from Malita: Matric Study Master, the study app for South African Grade 12 learners, provided by Malita (Pty) Ltd.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sbugzoh2@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Malita account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account", and include your full name as registered on the account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 business days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

Once your deletion request is processed, we permanently delete:

  • Your name, email address, password (hash), school, province, and city/town
  • Your South African ID number and date of birth
  • Your practice question history, solved questions, topics studied, and progress statistics
  • Your Collaboration Forum posts and replies
  • Login activity and session records

What we may retain, and for how long

A small amount of information may be retained beyond account deletion, only where we are required to by law or need it to meet a legitimate business obligation:

  • Billing/transaction records (e.g. subscription payment confirmations from PayFast or Google Play) — retained for up to 5 years to meet South African financial record-keeping and tax obligations.
  • Fraud/abuse prevention records, where relevant — retained only as long as necessary to protect the Service and other users.

We do not retain uploaded photos or PDF documents at all — these are processed to generate your answer and then discarded, whether or not you later delete your account (see our Privacy Policy for details).
