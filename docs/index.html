Built for South African learners and students

Malita is developed by Malita (Pty) Ltd, a South African company. Self-study AI tools follow the CAPS curriculum's exact conventions for Grade 12 Maths and Physical Sciences — degrees, not radians; South African terminology; real past exam questions. The live tutoring marketplace goes further, covering every CAPS subject from Grade 10-12 as well as university and college modules, through two apps built for where you study: Malita for school, Malita Campus for higher education.
